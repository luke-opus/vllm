Question: Is there an unmodified, publicly trained checkpoint at roughly
2 GB or less that reaches the modified RVV er() through a real CPU model
entry point, within a 4 GB QEMU guest / 16 GB host budget?

Finding: No suitable checkpoint was found among the candidates and built-in
model routes checked here. This is not an exhaustive search of Hugging Face
models, nor proof that no such checkpoint exists. No weights, activation
configuration, model dimensions or backend dispatch were changed for this audit.

CPU fused MoE exact GELU calls FP32Vec16.er():

Candidate exclusions

1. Moondream3 has exact GELU, but its fused route requires x.is_cuda.
   CPU falls back to an expert loop using torch.nn.functional.gelu.
   The official model card also lists 9B BF16 parameters (approximately
   18 GB of parameter storage before runtime overhead), outside this budget.
   Code:
   https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/ca8dbccf204abfcdf9dcb178f7e108c06b224b0f/vllm/model_executor/models/moondream3.py#L505-L521
   https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/ca8dbccf204abfcdf9dcb178f7e108c06b224b0f/vllm/model_executor/models/moondream3.py#L570-L573
   Official card: https://huggingface.co/moondream/moondream3-preview

2. Nomic Embed Text v2 MoE is a relevant small trained candidate: its official
   card gives 475M total parameters, and the config specifies float32 and
   GELU (approximately 1.9 GB of parameter storage, not a measured download).
   However, NomicMoE converts the activation to gelu_no_mul. That activation
   uses F.gelu, not the RVV er() under review. The model's direct fused_experts
   route is also distinct from CPUUnquantizedExperts; CPU model execution was
   not attempted or claimed. Running it would not establish this ERF coverage.
   Code:
   https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/ca8dbccf204abfcdf9dcb178f7e108c06b224b0f/vllm/model_executor/models/bert_with_rope.py#L255
   https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/ca8dbccf204abfcdf9dcb178f7e108c06b224b0f/vllm/model_executor/models/bert_with_rope.py#L332-L339
   https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/ca8dbccf204abfcdf9dcb178f7e108c06b224b0f/vllm/model_executor/layers/fused_moe/activation.py#L398-L399
   Official config: https://huggingface.co/nomic-ai/nomic-embed-text-v2-moe/raw/main/config.json
   Official card: https://huggingface.co/nomic-ai/nomic-embed-text-v2-moe

Validation boundary
The separate complete-extension and repository-test records reproduce the
exact-GELU numerical defect and its fix. The Pythia smoke model does not cover
that ERF path. This candidate audit does not close the changed-path trained-model
validation gap. Official model URLs above were read during this audit; their
main branches may change later, unlike the fixed source links.
